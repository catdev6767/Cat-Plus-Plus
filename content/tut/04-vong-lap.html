Repeat code with knead and groom.

knead

paw i = 1
knead i <= 5
    meow i
    paw i = i + 1

groom — browse list

paw cats = ["Tom", "Jerry", "Kitty"]
groom c of cats
    meow c

sit / leap

paw i = 0
knead i < 10
    paw i = i + 1
    sniff i == 3
        leap
    sniff i == 7
        sit
    meow i
