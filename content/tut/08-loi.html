Not let the program crash on errors.

Syntax

tap
    paw x = 1 / 0
hiss e
    meow "Error: " + e

Use

paw nums = [10, 0, 5]
groom n of nums
    tap
        meow 100 / n
    hiss e
        meow "Skip " + say(n)

Exercises

Cho ["10", "abc", "20"], try to shift the number, ignore the death penalty section.
